Understanding password attacks

Brute force attack

Systematically trying every possible combination of characters until the correct password is found. Longer passwords with a larger character pool make this exponentially harder.

Dictionary attack

Trying words from dictionaries and lists of commonly used passwords, rather than random combinations, because most people don't choose truly random passwords.

Credential stuffing

Using username/password pairs leaked from one breached service to try logging into other, unrelated services — exploiting password reuse.

Password spraying

Trying a small number of very common passwords (like "Summer2024!") against many different accounts, to avoid triggering account lockouts.

Rainbow tables

Precomputed tables that map password hashes back to plaintext passwords, letting an attacker reverse a stolen hash quickly instead of guessing from scratch.
